## Evidence sufficiency rule

For each finding, the auditor should retain enough objective evidence to allow another competent reviewer to understand:

1. what requirement was tested;
2. what evidence was examined;
3. what condition was observed;
4. why the condition matters;
5. how the finding was classified; and
6. what correction/corrective action is expected.
