## 2. Audit objective

Determine, through documented evidence and sampling, whether the assumed Conyers Capital ISMS:

- conforms to applicable ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements;
- is implemented and maintained as planned;
- appropriately addresses information-security risks;
- supports confidentiality, integrity, and availability;
- monitors information-security performance;
- identifies and addresses nonconformities; and
- supports continual improvement.

## 3. Audit scope

The simulated scope covers information and technology used by Conyers Capital for:

- acquisition target due diligence;
- financial and investment analysis;
- investor and lender communications;
- deal-team collaboration;
- document management;
- email and identity management;
- cloud/SaaS services;
- endpoint computing;
- third-party professional services;
- personnel onboarding/offboarding;
- incident response;
- business continuity and recovery.

### Scope boundary

Portfolio companies acquired or managed by Conyers Capital are treated as external entities unless explicitly included in a future ISMS scope statement.

Physical security of third-party data centers is treated as an externally provided service and assessed through supplier assurance rather than direct physical inspection.

## 4. Audit criteria

Primary criteria:

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clauses 4–10
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A, where applicable
- Conyers Capital information-security policies and procedures
- Risk-management methodology
- Contractual and regulatory obligations identified by the organization

## 5. Audit approach

The simulated audit uses:

- interviews;
- document review;
- observation;
- sample-based testing;
- control design assessment;
- control implementation assessment;
- risk-to-control traceability;
- evidence sufficiency testing.

A control is not considered effective merely because a policy exists. The simulated auditor looks for evidence that the control is implemented and operating.

## 8. Finding classification

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Major Nonconformity | A systemic or significant failure to meet a requirement, or a condition that materially undermines the ISMS |
| Minor Nonconformity | A localized failure that does not indicate total breakdown of the requirement |
| Observation | Evidence of potential weakness or inconsistency that does not currently support a nonconformity |
| Opportunity for Improvement | A practical improvement that could increase effectiveness or maturity |
| Conformity | Sufficient sampled evidence supports the criterion |

## 9. Audit conclusion format

The audit conclusion should be based on sampled evidence, not assumptions. Any final certification decision would belong to an independent certification body.

## 11. Independence note

For a real internal audit, auditors should be sufficiently independent of the activity being audited to support objectivity and impartiality. If the CEO is also the ISMS owner, an auditor should not audit their own work without appropriate safeguards.
