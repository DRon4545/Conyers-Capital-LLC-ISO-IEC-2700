## SoA governance

The SoA should be reviewed when:

- the risk assessment changes;
- significant technology or business changes occur;
- new legal/contractual requirements arise;
- new services or suppliers are introduced;
- incidents demonstrate a control gap;
- management changes risk treatment decisions.

The SoA is not merely a checklist; it should explain how applicability is determined and how selected controls relate to risk treatment.
