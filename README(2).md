# Conyers Capital — ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Internal Audit Portfolio

> **Fictional organization and fictional audit evidence.** This portfolio is an educational / portfolio artifact and is not evidence of certification, accreditation, or a completed independent certification audit.

## Organization

**Conyers Capital LLC** is a fictional private-equity acquisition company based in Connecticut.  
**CEO / Founder:** D'Ron Conyers

The assumed business model includes acquisition diligence, financial analysis, deal-team collaboration, investor communications, portfolio-company information exchange, and third-party professional services.

## Audit basis

This portfolio is structured as an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 internal audit using a GRC-style workflow:

1. Define scope and audit criteria
2. Identify processes and information assets
3. Establish audit objectives and sampling
4. Collect / document evidence
5. Test conformity
6. Record findings
7. Rate risk and priority
8. Assign corrective actions
9. Verify closure
10. Feed results into management review and continual improvement

The audit criteria are primarily ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clauses 4–10 and selected Annex A controls relevant to a private-equity environment.

This is a **simulated internal audit**. The organization has not been independently audited or certified based on the fictional evidence in this repository.

ISO/IEC 27001 certification, where pursued, is performed by an independent certification body; ISO itself does not perform certification.
